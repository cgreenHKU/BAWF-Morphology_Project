About This Database

This database comes from the largest study of figurative language in picturebooks to date. It contains over 68,000 examples of metaphors, similes, personification, and hyperbole from thousands of children's books.

Column Explanations:

  • Source Chain of Thought: AI explanation of the source domain (what familiar thing is being used to describe something)
  • Target Chain of Thought: AI explanation of the target domain (the abstract idea or emotion being described)
  • AI Short Comment: Quick quality rating + plain-language meaning
  • Find Source: Click to search Google for read-aloud videos of the book
