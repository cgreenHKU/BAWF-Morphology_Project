About this Database

  • Explore English word families, compound words, and definitions/etymologies

  • Morphology is the study of how words are built from meaningful units, including bases, roots, prefixes, and suffixes.
  • Filter or search for words you want and particular morphemes.
  • Use tabs and search box in the columns below to sort by frequency, grade level, or easily filter for morphologically related words
  • Paste word + frequency into the Word Cloud tab to make visuals
  • Use word families to build word walls
  • Use Zipf frequencies to sort for words in the 1st 1000 most frequent words, 2nd 1000 words etc
  • Download individual Excel files for offline use, advanced filtering, or editing. (Download tab is bottom right of spreadsheet)

Column Definitions for the BAWF Morphological Database

×

Core Word Information

  • Word: The surface wordform (as it appears in the dictionary and corpora)
  • Word_frq_Zipf: Zipf frequency score of the wordform, calculated from a large English corpus (higher value = more frequent in everyday language use).
  • FLemma: The lemma of the word (ignoring part of speech).
  • GPT_Coded_Word_Parts: Morphological breakdown of the word into prefixes, root(s), and suffixes.

Baseword / Root Information

  • Baseword: The central base form of the word after removing affixes.
  • Baseword_frq_Zipf: Zipf frequency score of the baseword itself.
  • Baseword_Family_Size: Number of wordforms in the same baseword family (excluding compounds).
  • Baseword_FamilySize_Incl_Compounds: Number of wordforms in the baseword family, including compounds.
  • Baseword_Zipf_Inc_Compounds: Zipf frequency of the baseword, adjusted to include compounds.

Root & Etymological Information

  • GPT_Root: Primary root identified using GPT-assisted analysis.
  • Avg_Root_Zipf_BNC_COCA: Average Zipf frequency of words sharing the same root.
  • Root_FamilySize: Number of different wordforms sharing the same root (excluding compounds).
  • Root_FamilySize_Inc_Compounds: Number of different wordforms sharing the same root, including compounds.
  • Avg_Root_Zipf_Inc_Compounds: Average Zipf frequency of words sharing the root, including compounds.

Greek / Latin Roots

  • Greek_Latin_Root_1: First identified classical (Greek or Latin) root.
  • Greek_Latin_Meaning_1: Meaning of the first classical root.
  • Greek_Latin_Root_1_FamilySize: Number of words containing this classical root.
  • Avg_Greek_Latin_Root_1_Zipf_BNC_COCA: Average Zipf frequency of words containing this root.
  • Greek_Latin_Root_2: Second identified classical root (if present).
  • Greek_Latin_Meaning_2: Meaning of the second classical root.
  • Greek_Latin_Roots_FamilySize_Incl_compounds: Total family size including compounds.
  • Greek_Latin_Roots_Zipf_Incl_compounds: Zipf measures for classical roots, including compounds.

Prefixes & Suffixes

  • Prefix_1 to Prefix_3 and Suffix_1 to Suffix_6: Include meaning, forms, frequency, count, and mean Zipf scores.

Other Morphological & Psycholinguistic Measures

  • N_Morphemes: Total number of morphemes in the word.
  • Reading AoA (Green et al., 2025): Age of Acquisition for reading.
  • Vocabulary eXpected Grade Level (Flor et al., 2024): Expected U.S. grade level.
  • GPT_Familiarity (Brysbaert et al., 2024): GPT-estimated familiarity rating.
